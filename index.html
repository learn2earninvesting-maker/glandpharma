The 90-second read

The biggest number nobody has modelled yet. A manufacturing and supply agreement was signed with a global specialty pharma company, disclosed for the first time on 10 Aug 2026, with potential annual revenue of $90–100m — about ₹860–955 cr, or 13–14% of everything the company sells today. No date has been given for when it starts. Q1FY27 call · 10 Aug 26

The first real money arrives in FY27 and is already visible. Making medicines for other companies (CDMO) is now half of all sales, and the profit left from each ₹100 of sales rose by ₹2.76 in the June quarter (margin, +276 bps).

What breaks it: this management has delivered only 6 of the 18 promises that have actually come due. Read every date here as one to two years later than stated.

Snapshot

what the business is

Gland makes injectable medicines — the kind that go into a drip or a syringe — mostly for other pharmaceutical companies, who then sell them under their own label. [F] It ships to 60-plus countries; the United States is 53–54% of sales. Business Overview · Jul 26

Where it sits in the chain: in the middle. [F] It runs its own plants making the raw active ingredients, then manufactures the finished injectable. 13 of the top 15 US generic injectable companies are customers, with relationships averaging over eight years.

Is it actually special, or just a commodity? [O] Genuinely special — and the proof is in the margin, not the marketing. Generic injectables are a commodity where they are finally sold, but Gland does not sell there. [F] It sells at a fixed price to a partner, so swings in raw-material costs and price wars land on the partner's slice, not Gland's. The real edge is its regulatory record: [F] no US FDA warning letter at any plant, ever. [O] That is what wins work from companies who cannot risk a shutdown — and it is exactly what one bad inspection would destroy. So: read these triggers as structural, not as a passing boom — but the edge is only one inspection deep.

The owner has been selling

[F] Fosun Pharma of Shanghai bought about 74% in 2017. It now holds 51.77%, having stepped down to 57.86% and then to 51.83% in a June 2024 block sale. BSE bulk deals · Jun 24

[F] Flat for eight quarters since, with zero shares pledged as security. SEBI filing · 2 Apr 26

[O] A controlling owner that has sold twice, and once publicly explored exiting altogether (2022), is a standing overhang on the share price — not a red flag about the business.

There is currently no CEO [F] Mr Shyamakant Giri resigned on 30 April 2026; a successor is being sought. Forensic Report · Aug 26

Risks to the trigger thesis

what breaks these specific triggers
  • The delivery record undercuts every medium-conviction trigger — hits 1, 4 and 6 [F] Of 29 tracked promises, 18 have come due: 6 delivered, 6 partly delivered, 6 missed. Guidance Report · Jul 26 Misses include Sputnik V production, China filings (7 of 12), enoxaparin volumes and FY22 US growth. [O] Slippage of a year or more is the base case here, not the downside case.
  • The edge is one FDA letter deep, and there is smoke — hits 1, 2 and 5 at once [F] A Form 483 with three observations was issued at the Visakhapatnam plant in February 2025, and the French regulator issued 11 observations at Cenexi Fontenay in June 2025. Forensic Report · Aug 26 [O] Both were procedural and neither disrupted operations — but the entire contract-manufacturing thesis rests on a spotless record.
  • Sales are turning into unpaid bills, and one customer is getting large [F] Customers now take 107 days to pay, up from 99. Money owed by customers grew 127% over five years to ₹1,520.69 cr, and one customer alone is 20.15% of it. Revenue recognition has been the auditor's flagged focus area every year from FY21 to FY26. Forensic Report · Aug 26 [O] Growth that arrives as unpaid bills rather than cash is the specific way a contract-manufacturing ramp can look better than it is.
  • Cenexi goodwill is being tested against harder assumptions with nothing written off — hits 4 [F] ₹2,938.70m of goodwill sits entirely on the Phixen unit, which has been loss-making at the operating level. The discount rate used to test it rose from 10.3% to 12.6% while assumed long-run growth was cut from 2.5% to 2.0% — and no write-down has ever been taken. Next test: 31 March 2027. Forensic Report · Aug 26
  • There is no CEO — and this is the most likely way the dates move again [F] Mr Shyamakant Giri resigned on 30 April 2026 and a successor is being sought. [O] A ₹2,000 cr building programme and a new flagship contract are both being run under interim command. Whoever arrives may reset the guidance.
